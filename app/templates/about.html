About the system

An explainable, symptom-based expert system

CropCare Expert compares farmer-selected symptoms with a knowledge base of crop diseases and IF-THEN production rules.

How the rules work

Each rule lists required and supporting symptoms. The system reports matched rules, weighted symptom coverage, possible diseases, and cases where evidence is not strong enough.

Images are documentation only

Uploaded crop photographs are not analyzed or sent to an image-recognition model. They are stored with the diagnosis record for the farmer's reference.

Results are decision support, not a laboratory-confirmed diagnosis. Seek local agricultural advice when symptoms are severe or unclear.

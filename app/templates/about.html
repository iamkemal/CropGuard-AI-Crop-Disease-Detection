About CropGuard

Practical guidance.
Clear reasoning.

CropGuard supports maize and tomato growers in Bosaso with symptom-based disease guidance. Each result shows the observations and rules behind it.

01

Observe

Inspect the plant and choose only symptoms you can identify. Clear descriptions help you record what you see.

02

Compare

The system compares your selections with stored IF-THEN rules and ranks possible disease matches.

03

Decide

Review evidence and care guidance. Consult a local agricultural adviser when signs are severe or unclear.

{{ notice('Photos are optional records. Disease matching uses your symptom selections. A rule-match score describes coverage of stored rules and does not confirm a diagnosis.', 'warning') }}

How the rules work

Each rule lists required and supporting symptoms. CropGuard explains the matched rules, weighted symptom coverage, and cases where more evidence is needed.

Images are documentation only

Uploaded photographs and camera pictures are not analyzed or sent to an image-recognition model. They stay with the private diagnosis record for your reference.
